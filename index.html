      Both Homes

      A calm, private way to keep two homes on the same page. Built to be understood at a glance, printed without fuss, and owned by the people using it.

      Private by design

      There are no accounts and no analytics. Your names, dates and notes are saved with localStorage on this device only. Share links keep the plan in the link itself — nothing is uploaded, and anyone holding the link can read it.

      What is stored, and how to clear it

      Your plan is one local record. The theme choice is another. Both can be removed with . Exported files and share links are copies you control.

      Use it with care

      Both Homes is a planning tool, not legal advice. Follow your court order or parenting agreement. In an emergency, contact the appropriate local services rather than relying on a calendar.

      Calendar exports use floating local times, so each app applies its own timezone.

      Calendar export

      Send this plan to a calendar

      Each file contains only that parent’s nights, with handoff details in the event notes. Import it into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook, or anything that reads .ics.

      Times are written as local wall-clock times, so each calendar app uses the timezone of the device importing it. Re-importing a new file can create duplicates; remove the old calendar first if you update it.

      Share

      Send the plan as a link

      The whole plan is encoded in the link. Nothing is uploaded, no account is needed, and the link opens a working copy of the calendar.

      What to include in the link

      Anyone with the link can read the included details. Leave names and notes off when sharing in a group or with a mediator.
